Xero · Non-GST-registered business

BrightPath Creative Studio — non-GST full-quarter bookkeeping

Sole trader · Creative services · Quarter ended 30 September 2025

Managed a full quarter of Xero bookkeeping for BrightPath Creative Studio — a graphic design studio below the $75,000 GST threshold and not registered for GST. Recorded 10 client invoices for design services (UrbanNest, EcoGlow, Bloom & Co.), processed all supplier bills (rent, software subscriptions, utilities, insurance), posted 3 months of payroll journals with PAYG withholding and superannuation entries, and performed a complete Westpac bank reconciliation. All 54 journal entries balanced with debits equal to credits, and the bank account reconciled to a closing balance of –$37,903.98 with zero outstanding items.
